About Me

Welcome to My Online Space!

Greetings! I'm a Mumbai-based analytics and tech enthusiast with a flair for data and a passion for innovation. With over four years of experience, I've carved my niche as a Business Analyst, Risk Analyst, and BI Developer working with renowned organizations like Barclays, Edelweiss Tokio Life Insurance, and Datamatics. My passion lies in creating data-driven solutions that enhance decision-making, mitigate risks, and drive innovation.

During a career break, I embraced resilience and pursued my interests in financial engineering and quantitative systematic trading. This period allowed me to develop a fully automated proprietary trading system using Python and Power BI. Currently, I’m actively involved in research and development of modeling systems for systematic trading in the Indian equity markets.

In my recent roles, I've been instrumental in implementing data-driven solutions to streamline operations, optimize processes, and enhance decision-making. Leveraging a diverse toolkit of ML & Analytics frameworks, including R Studio, Python, Tableau, and Power BI, I've led projects aimed at automating reporting processes, improving data quality management, and detecting and mitigating risks.

I hold certifications in Microsoft Power BI, Algorithmic Trading, Lean Six Sigma, and Advanced SQL, reflecting my commitment to continuous learning. These skills complement my educational background—an MBA in Business Operations & Analytics and a Bachelor's in Computer Science.

Beyond work, I find joy in yoga, trekking, and exploring culinary arts—activities that fuel my creativity and well-being.

Feel free to explore my projects on GitHub or reach out for collaboration, mentorship, or discussion. Let’s create impactful solutions and achieve growth together!
